The art of the long lunch

Set the table in the shade an hour before anyone arrives. Bring the cushions out, open a bottle, and let the terrace warm up around you. The point is not the menu but the length of the afternoon.

Low chairs keep conversation close. A daybed at the edge gives the slowest guest somewhere to stay when the plates are cleared. Nothing needs to match; it only needs to invite you to sit a little longer.

When the light turns gold, leave everything where it is. That is what pieces made for outside are for.
